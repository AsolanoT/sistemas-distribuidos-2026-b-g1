Agile Methodologies

Approaches to managing projects that prioritize incremental delivery, flexibility to change, and continuous collaboration, instead of planning everything up front (like waterfall).

Scrum

An iterative and incremental framework based on cycles called Sprints (1–4 weeks). A small cross-functional team delivers a functional increment at the end of each sprint.

Kanban

A visual workflow method, without sprints. It focuses on visualizing tasks on a board, limiting work in progress (WIP) and improving continuous delivery.
